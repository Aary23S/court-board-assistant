# Final Board Template

This document defines the structural template of the final board, independent of the business rules that classify cases into these sections.

## Concept: Template vs Classification

**Final Board Template**:
Determines *which sections exist* and *which stage rows (labels) are visible under them*.
A canonical stage can explicitly appear as a row in multiple sections (e.g., `Dismissal Order` appears under Hearing, M.A., D.V., R.C.C., and S.C.C.).

**Case Classification Rules**:
Determine *where a specific case goes* (e.g. Case #123 with stage `Dismissal Order` belongs in `D.V.`). These rules are provided by the officers and managed separately.
